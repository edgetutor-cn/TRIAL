L0_U05_L04 · Snack Time: Play and Use 1

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: apple, milk

Main frame: Can I have ___, please?

Review pool: apple, banana, bread, milk, carrot, water

Core models

apple — Can I have an apple, please?

banana — Can I have a banana, please?

bread — Can I have some bread, please?

milk — Can I have some milk, please?

carrot — Can I have a carrot, please?

water — Can I have some water, please?

Extension

orange — I'd like an orange, please.

juice — I'd like some juice, please.

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U05_L04-P01 · Snack Time: Play and Use 1 · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U05_L04-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. Say milk, water, bread, rice and cheese alone. Do not add a or an before these words. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U05_L04-P03 · Our familiar song! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 5 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U05_L04-P04 · Getting ready together. · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Snack time. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U05_L04-P05 · The picture theatre. · 1 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U05_L04-P06 · Make our picture book! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U05_L04-P07 · Spin and say! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U05_L04-P08 · Watch, listen, take a turn. · 1.5 min

Model: Can I have an apple, please? Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U05_L04-P09 · Find our picture! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U05_L04-P10 · A quiet little hiding game. · 2 min

Show and name both pictures, then tuck one away. Ask for the missing picture; use Show both as support. No timer and no wrong-answer penalty.

L0_U05_L04-P11 · A wave and a seated wiggle. · 1 min

Model: Can I have some bread, please? Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U05_L04-P13 · A page for our friends. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U05_L04-P14 · Our friends need your help. · 2 min

Set the scene: Snack time. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U05_L04-P15 · Little jobs, one happy party. · 1.5 min

One pair at a time. Say the target and help our friends complete the little task. Six or twelve words never appear at once. Say milk, water, bread, rice and cheese alone. Do not add a or an before these words.

L0_U05_L04-P18 · Is there time for one more little game? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U05_L04-P19 · Goodbye, party friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U05_L04-P21 · Explore: orange · 1 min

Introduce orange using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I'd like an orange, please. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U05_L04-P22 · Explore: juice · 1 min

Introduce juice using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I'd like some juice, please. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U05_L04-P23 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

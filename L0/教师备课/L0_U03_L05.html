L0_U03_L05 · Cow and pig

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: cow, pig

Main frame: This is a ___.

Review pool:

Core models

cow — This is a cow.

pig — This is a pig.

Extension

frog — Can you find the frog?

bear — Can you find the bear?

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U03_L05-P01 · Cow and pig · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U03_L05-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. Animal names are for pointing and pretending. No sound effect or animal fact is a word test. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U03_L05-P03 · Sing and move! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 3 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U03_L05-P04 · A little surprise! · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Animal garden. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U03_L05-P05 · Open our little theatre. · 1.5 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U03_L05-P06 · Learn: cow · 1.5 min

Model twice: This is a cow. Show the picture, then your own example. A point, gesture or approximate word is enough.

L0_U03_L05-P07 · Watch, copy, take a turn. · 1 min

Model: This is a cow. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U03_L05-P08 · One more little moment. · 1 min

L0_U03_L05-P09 · Learn: pig · 0.5 min

L0_U03_L05-P17 · You are our little teacher! · 1 min

L0_U03_L05-P18 · Our little picture book. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U03_L05-P19 · More time for a little adventure? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U03_L05-P20 · Goodbye, little friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U03_L05-P22 · Explore: frog · 1 min

Introduce frog using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: Can you find the frog? Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U03_L05-P23 · Explore: bear · 1 min

L0_U03_L05-P24 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

L0_U02_L08 · My Caring Family: Play and Use 2

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: Grandma, sister

Main frame: I love my ___.

Review pool: Mum, Dad, Grandma, Grandpa, sister, brother, baby, me, aunt, uncle, hug, kiss

Core models

Mum — I love my Mum.

Dad — I love my Dad.

Grandma — I love my Grandma.

Grandpa — I love my Grandpa.

sister — I love my sister.

brother — I love my brother.

baby — I love my baby.

me — This is me.

aunt — I love my aunt.

uncle — I love my uncle.

hug — I can give a hug.

kiss — I can give a kiss.

Extension

friend — I like my friend.

teacher — I like my teacher.

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U02_L08-P01 · My Caring Family: Play and Use 2 · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U02_L08-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. This is our story family. Do not guess a role from hair or clothes, and do not ask about the child's own family. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U02_L08-P03 · Our familiar song! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 2 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U02_L08-P04 · Getting ready together. · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Our family album. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U02_L08-P05 · The picture theatre. · 1 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U02_L08-P06 · Find our picture! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U02_L08-P18 · Is there time for one more little game? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U02_L08-P19 · Goodbye, party friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U02_L08-P21 · Explore: friend · 1 min

Introduce friend using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I like my friend. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U02_L08-P22 · Explore: teacher · 1 min

Introduce teacher using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I like my teacher. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U02_L08-P23 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

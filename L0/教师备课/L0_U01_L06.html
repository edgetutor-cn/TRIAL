L0_U01_L06 · Arms and legs

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: arms, legs

Main frame: These are my ___.

Review pool:

Core models

arms — These are my arms.

legs — These are my legs.

Extension

finger — I can touch my finger.

toes — I can touch my toes.

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U01_L06-P01 · Arms and legs · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U01_L06-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. Use plural forms naturally (eyes, ears, hands, feet, arms, legs). These are my eyes is fine; do not test a sentence. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U01_L06-P03 · Sing and move! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 1 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U01_L06-P04 · A little surprise! · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Mirror time. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U01_L06-P05 · Open our little theatre. · 1.5 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U01_L06-P06 · Learn: arms · 1.5 min

Model twice: These are my arms. Show the picture, then your own example. A point, gesture or approximate word is enough.

L0_U01_L06-P07 · Watch, copy, take a turn. · 1 min

Model: These are my arms. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U01_L06-P08 · One more little moment. · 1 min

L0_U01_L06-P17 · You are our little teacher! · 1 min

The learner chooses a picture to share with a friend. Model the matching sentence and invite a real example. Personal choices accept either option.

L0_U01_L06-P18 · Our little picture book. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U01_L06-P19 · More time for a little adventure? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U01_L06-P20 · Goodbye, little friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U01_L06-P22 · Explore: finger · 1 min

Introduce finger using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I can touch my finger. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U01_L06-P23 · Explore: toes · 1 min

Introduce toes using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I can touch my toes. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U01_L06-P24 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

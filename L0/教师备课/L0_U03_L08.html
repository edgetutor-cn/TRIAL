L0_U03_L08 · Animal Neighbours: Play and Use 2

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: dog, horse

Main frame: I can see a ___ here.

Review pool: cat, dog, duck, fish, bird, rabbit, cow, pig, sheep, goat, turtle, horse

Core models

cat — I can see a cat here.

dog — I can see a dog here.

duck — I can see a duck here.

fish — I can see a fish here.

bird — I can see a bird here.

rabbit — I can see a rabbit here.

cow — I can see a cow here.

pig — I can see a pig here.

sheep — I can see a sheep here.

goat — I can see a goat here.

turtle — I can see a turtle here.

horse — I can see a horse here.

Extension

frog — Can you find the frog?

bear — Can you find the bear?

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U03_L08-P01 · Animal Neighbours: Play and Use 2 · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U03_L08-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. Animal names are for pointing and pretending. No sound effect or animal fact is a word test. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U03_L08-P03 · Our familiar song! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 3 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U03_L08-P04 · Getting ready together. · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Animal garden. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U03_L08-P05 · The picture theatre. · 1 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U03_L08-P06 · Listen and find! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U03_L08-P07 · Catch a picture! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U03_L08-P08 · Watch, listen, take a turn. · 1.5 min

Model: I can see a duck here. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U03_L08-P09 · Find and say! · 1.5 min

Show the picture, model the sentence, then invite a choice. These source pictures are opaque, so silhouette matching is intentionally not used.

L0_U03_L08-P10 · A quiet little hiding game. · 2 min

Show and name both pictures, then tuck one away. Ask for the missing picture; use Show both as support. No timer and no wrong-answer penalty.

L0_U03_L08-P11 · A wave and a seated wiggle. · 1 min

Model: I can see a bird here. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U03_L08-P12 · Make our picture book! · 2 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U03_L08-P13 · A page for our friends. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U03_L08-P14 · Our friends need your help. · 2 min

Set the scene: Animal garden. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U03_L08-P15 · Little jobs, one happy party. · 1.5 min

One pair at a time. Say the target and help our friends complete the little task. Six or twelve words never appear at once. Animal names are for pointing and pretending. No sound effect or animal fact is a word test.

L0_U03_L08-P16 · You lead our party! · 1 min

Model: I can see a cat here. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U03_L08-P17 · Our party picture book. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U03_L08-P18 · Is there time for one more little game? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U03_L08-P19 · Goodbye, party friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U03_L08-P21 · Explore: frog · 1 min

Introduce frog using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: Can you find the frog? Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U03_L08-P22 · Explore: bear · 1 min

Introduce bear using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: Can you find the bear? Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U03_L08-P23 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

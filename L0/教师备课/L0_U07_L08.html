L0_U07_L08 · Fun Together: Play and Use 2

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: sing, sandbox

Main frame: Let's ___ together.

Review pool: sing, dance, draw, read, drum, guitar, slide, swing, seesaw, sandbox, bubbles, balloons

Core models

sing — Let's sing together.

dance — Let's dance together.

draw — Let's draw together.

read — Let's read together.

drum — Let's play the drum together.

guitar — Let's play the guitar together.

slide — Let's slide together.

swing — Let's swing together.

seesaw — Let's play on the seesaw together.

sandbox — Let's play in the sandbox together.

bubbles — Let's play with bubbles together.

balloons — Let's play with balloons together.

Extension

song — Can we sing a song together?

piano — I can play the piano.

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U07_L08-P01 · Fun Together: Play and Use 2 · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U07_L08-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. Here read means looking at a picture book and draw means free colouring. No reading or writing task is required. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U07_L08-P03 · Our familiar song! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 7 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U07_L08-P04 · Getting ready together. · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Let’s have fun. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U07_L08-P05 · The picture theatre. · 1 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U07_L08-P06 · Make our picture book! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U07_L08-P07 · Spin and say! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U07_L08-P08 · Watch, listen, take a turn. · 1.5 min

Model: Let's draw together. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U07_L08-P09 · Pop up, little picture! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U07_L08-P10 · A quiet little hiding game. · 2 min

Show and name both pictures, then tuck one away. Ask for the missing picture; use Show both as support. No timer and no wrong-answer penalty.

L0_U07_L08-P11 · A wave and a seated wiggle. · 1 min

Model: Let's play the drum together. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U07_L08-P13 · A page for our friends. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U07_L08-P14 · Our friends need your help. · 2 min

Set the scene: Let’s have fun. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U07_L08-P15 · Little jobs, one happy party. · 1.5 min

One pair at a time. Say the target and help our friends complete the little task. Six or twelve words never appear at once. Here read means looking at a picture book and draw means free colouring. No reading or writing task is required.

Model: Let's sing together. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U07_L08-P18 · Is there time for one more little game? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U07_L08-P19 · Goodbye, party friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U07_L08-P21 · Explore: song · 1 min

Introduce song using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: Can we sing a song together? Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U07_L08-P22 · Explore: piano · 1 min

Introduce piano using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I can play the piano. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U07_L08-P23 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

L0_U04_L04 · My Play Basket: Play and Use 1

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: ball, train

Main frame: I have ___ for you.

Review pool: ball, car, teddy, doll, blocks, train

Core models

ball — I have a ball for you.

car — I have a car for you.

teddy — I have a teddy for you.

doll — I have a doll for you.

blocks — I have some blocks for you.

train — I have a train for you.

Extension

truck — Put the truck in the box.

bike — Can I have the bike, please?

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U04_L04-P01 · My Play Basket: Play and Use 1 · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U04_L04-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. The toy pictures are for choosing and giving. No real object is needed on the child side. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U04_L04-P03 · Our familiar song! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 4 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U04_L04-P04 · Getting ready together. · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Our toy corner. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U04_L04-P05 · The picture theatre. · 1 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U04_L04-P06 · Find and say! · 1.5 min

Show the picture, model the sentence, then invite a choice. These source pictures are opaque, so silhouette matching is intentionally not used.

L0_U04_L04-P07 · What is missing? · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U04_L04-P08 · Watch, listen, take a turn. · 1.5 min

Model: I have a ball for you. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U04_L04-P09 · Listen and find! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U04_L04-P10 · A quiet little hiding game. · 2 min

Show and name both pictures, then tuck one away. Ask for the missing picture; use Show both as support. No timer and no wrong-answer penalty.

L0_U04_L04-P11 · A wave and a seated wiggle. · 1 min

Model: I have a teddy for you. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U04_L04-P12 · Spin and say! · 2 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U04_L04-P13 · A page for our friends. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U04_L04-P14 · Our friends need your help. · 2 min

Set the scene: Our toy corner. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U04_L04-P15 · Little jobs, one happy party. · 1.5 min

One pair at a time. Say the target and help our friends complete the little task. Six or twelve words never appear at once. The toy pictures are for choosing and giving. No real object is needed on the child side.

L0_U04_L04-P16 · You lead our party! · 1 min

Model: I have a ball for you. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U04_L04-P17 · Our party picture book. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U04_L04-P18 · Is there time for one more little game? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U04_L04-P19 · Goodbye, party friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U04_L04-P21 · Explore: truck · 1 min

Introduce truck using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: Put the truck in the box. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U04_L04-P22 · Explore: bike · 1 min

Introduce bike using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: Can I have the bike, please? Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U04_L04-P23 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

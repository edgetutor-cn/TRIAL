L0_U08_L01 · Clap and wave

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: clap, wave

Main frame: I can ___ and ___.

Review pool:

Core models

clap — I can clap and wave.

wave — I can wave and clap.

Extension

tiptoe — I can tiptoe.

march — I can march.

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U08_L01-P01 · Clap and wave · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U08_L01-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. A seated action, finger action or puppet move is enough. Never race and never ask the child to leave the seat. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U08_L01-P03 · Sing and move! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 8 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U08_L01-P04 · A little surprise! · 1.5 min

Set the scene: Move with me. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U08_L01-P05 · Open our little theatre. · 1.5 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U08_L01-P06 · Learn: clap · 1.5 min

Model twice: I can clap and wave. Show the picture, then your own example. A point, gesture or approximate word is enough.

L0_U08_L01-P07 · Watch, copy, take a turn. · 1 min

Model: I can clap and wave. Let the child point or imitate. Wait 3–5 seconds, switch roles, then ask for a real example. Speaking is teacher observed, not machine scored.

L0_U08_L01-P08 · One more little moment. · 1 min

Set the scene: Move with me. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U08_L01-P09 · Learn: wave · 0.5 min

Model twice: I can wave and clap. Show the picture, then your own example. A point, gesture or approximate word is enough.

L0_U08_L01-P10 · Listen and find! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U08_L01-P17 · You are our little teacher! · 1 min

The learner chooses a picture to share with a friend. Model the matching sentence and invite a real example. Personal choices accept either option.

L0_U08_L01-P18 · Our little picture book. · 1.5 min

Choose the requested picture and place it into our little book. Point to the real thing or show the gesture afterwards. This is communication, not a reading task.

L0_U08_L01-P19 · More time for a little adventure? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U08_L01-P20 · Goodbye, little friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U08_L01-P22 · Explore: tiptoe · 1 min

Introduce tiptoe using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I can tiptoe. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U08_L01-P23 · Explore: march · 1 min

Introduce march using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I can march. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U08_L01-P24 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.

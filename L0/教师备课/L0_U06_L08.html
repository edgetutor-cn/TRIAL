L0_U06_L08 · How I Feel: Play and Use 2

Age 3–5 · Level 0 · 25-minute core plan · English

Core focus: happy, sad

Main frame: Today I feel ___, not ___.

Review pool: happy, sad, angry, sleepy, hungry, thirsty, scared, okay, hot, cold, tired, excited

Core models

happy — I feel happy today.

sad — I feel sad today.

angry — I feel angry today.

sleepy — I feel sleepy today.

hungry — I am hungry now.

thirsty — I am thirsty now.

scared — I feel scared today.

okay — I feel okay today.

hot — I am hot now.

cold — I am cold now.

tired — I feel tired today.

excited — I feel excited today.

Extension

calm — I feel calm when I rest.

surprised — I feel surprised today.

Teaching plan

The minutes guide teacher pacing, not a timer. Model → supported response → swap roles → real example. Accept pointing or a gesture while building towards the main sentence. Do not require reading or writing.

L0_U06_L08-P01 · How I Feel: Play and Use 2 · 0.5 min

Show the topic, the two focus pictures and the model. Then open Say hello. This is not a reading test.

L0_U06_L08-P02 · Say hello! · 1 min

Teacher-led, away from the screen: model one turn, wait 3-5 seconds, then let the child lead. Each button changes the classroom turn, it does not score the child. Every feeling is welcome. A child's own feeling has no right or wrong answer; this game checks a picture choice. Model "Hello, Jojo" and "Hello, Mimi" with a wave.

L0_U06_L08-P03 · Our familiar song! · 1.5 min

Play our Unit 6 song once, on demand. It is a warm-up, not a vocabulary test. Follow one easy action; pause while you speak.

L0_U06_L08-P04 · Getting ready together. · 1.5 min

Set the scene: How do you feel?. Show one picture, model its sentence, wait, then reveal the other. Ask the learner to choose or show a real example. Swap who leads; do not claim that a picture click proves speaking.

L0_U06_L08-P05 · The picture theatre. · 1 min

Open one curtain, model the pictured word with its sentence, close it, then let the learner choose the other. Support a point, a gesture or a spoken response.

L0_U06_L08-P06 · Make our picture book! · 1.5 min

Teacher models the current visible sentence, waits for a choice, then asks the learner to use the same frame. Repeat with the other target. Reset or advance the round to change pictures. Feelings and personal preferences are open responses.

L0_U06_L08-P18 · Is there time for one more little game? · 0 min

The core 25-minute plan ends here. Use extension only if time remains. Both routes are positive endings.

L0_U06_L08-P19 · Goodbye, party friends! · 1 min

Briefly celebrate one observed action, play the supplied goodbye song, and wave together. Do not invent independent mastery.

L0_U06_L08-P21 · Explore: calm · 1 min

Introduce calm using its matching picture and one real demonstration. Model: I feel calm when I rest. Do not test an unintroduced word. Offer support, swap roles, and leave for the goodbye song whenever time is nearly up.

L0_U06_L08-P23 · Your new words! · 1 min

Revisit the two new words one at a time. The child chooses which example to use; teacher supplies the model, then fades help. Never display six or twelve new labels at once.

Use and sound

Words and sentences are teacher-led. Four manual celebrations have different effects and audio. Share computer sound when teaching. Stop with the Goodbye song; the teacher panel never opens automatically. Picture choices and rewards are not speaking scores.
